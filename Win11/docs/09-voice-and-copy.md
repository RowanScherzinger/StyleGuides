# Voice and copy *(Proposed)*

Write like Windows: warm, plain, brief. Follow the Microsoft Writing Style Guide where this is silent.

- **Sentence case** for everything, including titles and buttons.
- **Talk to the user as "you"**. Contractions are fine ("can't", "you'll").
- **Buttons say what they do**: "Copy 12 photos", "Delete", "Save". Not "OK" when a verb fits.
- **Short**: lead with what matters. A setting's description is one line.
- **Plain, not playful**: no exclamation marks, jokes, emoji or hearts. "Copy complete", not "Yay!".
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do next: "Couldn't copy 3 photos. The phone was
  disconnected. Connect it and try again." Never blame the user. No error codes in the
  headline; put them in details.
- **No "please"** in instructions, and no "Are you sure?". Say what will happen: "Delete
  12 photos from the phone? You can't undo this."
- **Names**: the UI's own words for things. "Settings", not "Preferences" or "Options".
