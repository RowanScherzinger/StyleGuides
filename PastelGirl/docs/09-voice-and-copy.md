# Voice and copy *(Proposed)*

Very cute (decisions/0009): warm, playful and encouraging. Like a friend, not a toddler.

- Sentence case for everything, including buttons.
- Buttons say what happens: "Save recipe", "Delete list".
- Success is cheerful: "Yay, saved!", "All done ♥", "You did it!".
- Empty states are sweet: "Nothing here yet. Shall we add your first one?"
- Waiting is gentle: "Just a moment…", "Syncing your photos".
- Errors stay clear: say what went wrong and what to do. A soft opener is fine, the facts come
  right after: "Oops, that key didn't work. Check it and try again."
- Destructive confirmations are plain. No cute words next to "delete".
- Little text hearts and sparkles (♥ ✦) are fine in success and empty-state copy. No emoji.
- Numbers: thousands separators, units after a thin space ("1,240 g").
