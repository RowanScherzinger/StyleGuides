# 0009. Very cute

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: accepted

## Decision
The guide aims for very cute, not "cute balanced with cool". Hearts and moons for status are
right. Motifs, bouncy reactions and playful copy are encouraged.

## Why
The user's answer when asked whether hearts and stars were too cute for status: "We want it to
be very cute." The reference article's cute-vs-cool balance is kept only as an optional
silver detail.

## Affects
`docs/00-principles.md`, `docs/05-motion.md` (wiggle, boop), `docs/06-iconography.md`,
`docs/09-voice-and-copy.md`, `css/soft.css`, decision 0010.
