# 0002. Pastel girl direction

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: accepted

## Decision
A softer, rounder, pastel style that's feminine but dark. Lots of pinks and creams.

## Why
The user's brief, with the pastel e-girl look as the reference
([Kawaii Vibe](https://kawaiivibe.com/blogs/kawaii/pastel-egirl-all-you-need-to-know)).
A second reference, a Dribbble educational app concept, is listed in `docs/00-principles.md`
but not yet reviewed.

The guide was first named Dark Pastel Girl and renamed Pastel Girl the same day, when a light
variant was added (0008). Dark stays the default.

## Affects
Everything. In particular the pink and cream ramps and the plum field in `tokens/tokens.css`.
