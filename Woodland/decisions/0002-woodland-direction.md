# 0002. Woodland direction

Date: 2026-10-24
Status: accepted

## Decision
A style guide named Woodland: soft, earthy and natural, with green, forest colours and
forest items and icons.

## Why
The user's brief. Everything about how to get there (palette values, variants, type, shapes,
motifs, motion) is proposed in later decisions.

## Affects
Everything. In particular the moss, oat, bark and pine ramps in `tokens/tokens.css` and the
motifs in `css/woodland.css`.
