# Voice and copy *(Proposed)*

Calm, warm and plain, like a friendly ranger. Gentle, never twee.

- Sentence case for everything, including buttons.
- Buttons say what happens: "Save recipe", "Delete list".
- Success is quiet: "Recipe saved.", "All done."
- Empty states can lean on the theme, lightly: "Nothing growing here yet. Plant your first one?"
- Waiting is patient: "Gathering your photos…", "Just a moment…".
- Errors say what went wrong and what to do, with no theme words: "Sign-in failed: the key was
  rejected. Check it and try again."
- Destructive confirmations are plain.
- No emoji. Motifs do the decorating.
- Numbers: thousands separators, units after a thin space ("1,240 g").
