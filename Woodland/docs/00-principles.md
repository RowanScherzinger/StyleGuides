# Principles

The overall feel. When a specific rule is missing, decide by these.

## Direction

Soft, earthy and natural, in green, forest colours, with forest items and icons
(decisions/0002). Light by default like a sunlit clearing, dark for the forest at night
(decisions/0003, proposed).

## Feel *(Proposed)*

| Quality | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Soft** | Rounded corners, blurred shadows, low contrast between surfaces. Nothing glossy or sharp. |
| **Earthy** | Oat paper, bark ink, moss and walnut. Colours look like things you could pick up on a walk. |
| **Natural** | A faint paper grain, dappled light in the corners, slightly uneven pebble shapes. |
| **Calm** | Slow, swaying motion. One thing leads per card; the rest is quiet. Roomy spacing. |
| **Of the forest** | Leaves, ferns, pines, mushrooms, acorns and pinecones as small decoration. |

## Do *(Proposed)*

- Lead with moss on oat. Most screens need nothing else.
- Use bark brown for text in light, never black. Use oat in dark, never white.
- Add at most two guest colours (walnut, fern, lake, lichen, heather) per screen as decoration.
  Small status glyphs don't count.
- Give a card one small motif: a leaf by the title, a mushroom in the empty state, a faint fern
  in the corner of the hero card.
- Keep motion slow and gentle: things grow in and sway; nothing bounces.

## Don't *(Proposed)*

- No pure white or pure black, and no neon or saturated greens.
- No sharp corners (under 6px) on anything bigger than a checkbox.
- No hard drop shadows with offsets.
- No rowan red except for danger.
- No green status without its glyph and words: moss and fern are both green.
- Don't reuse status glyphs as decoration, or motifs as status (see `06-iconography.md`).
- No photographic textures (wood grain, bark photos). The grain is a faint noise, not a picture.
